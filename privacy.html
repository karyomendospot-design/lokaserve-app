Privacy Policy — LokaServe

Effective Date: November 2026

LokaServe respects user privacy. We only request Pi Network username and payment scope via the official Pi SDK to authenticate users and process local service bookings.

Booking details (name, WhatsApp contact, address, and visit schedule) are used solely to connect customers with their chosen local service provider. We never ask for your Pi wallet passphrase or sell personal data to third parties.
